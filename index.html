Our Story

Hot, Ready & Spicy

"Salaama" means peace and welcome in Somali, Arabic, and Swahili alike — the feeling we want every guest to leave with. It's the name on our door, and the promise behind everything we serve.

From fire-grilled ribs, shanks and chicken to our bubble tea and coffee bar, fresh smoothies, and made-to-order desserts — Salaama Eats brings it all together under one roof. Hot, ready, and always a little spicy.
