Our Story

Where the Mediterranean meets the modern table.

Salama Eats Ltd began with a simple belief: that halal food deserves a truly elegant table. Every dish is built on Mediterranean tradition — charcoal-grilled meats, house-blended spices, scratch-made hummus and tahini — plated with the precision of a contemporary kitchen.

"Salama" means peace and safety. It's the feeling we want every guest to leave with: nourished, unhurried, and cared for.
