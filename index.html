Our Heritage

A Table Built Along the Trade Routes

"Salama" carries the same meaning in Somali, Arabic, and Swahili — peace, safety, welcome. It's a word that has greeted travellers along coastal trade routes for centuries, from the shores of the Horn of Africa to the ports of Arabia and the Mediterranean.

We built this restaurant as a tribute to that shared table — Andalusian courtyards and citrus groves, Levantine mezze, and the generous, unhurried hospitality of Somali & Arab tradition. Every dish carries a little of each.
